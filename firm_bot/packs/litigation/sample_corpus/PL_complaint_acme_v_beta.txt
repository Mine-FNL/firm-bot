FIRST AMENDED COMPLAINT FOR FRAUD AND BREACH OF CONTRACT

    Plaintiff ACME CORP ("Plaintiff"), by and through its undersigned

                                JURISDICTION AND VENUE

1.  This Court has subject matter jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C.

    claims occurred in this District.

3.  This action arises under the laws of the United States and the

    supplemental jurisdiction over the state-law claims under 28
    U.S.C. § 1367.

                                    THE PARTIES

4.  Plaintiff ACME CORP is a [REDACTED STATE] corporation with its
    principal place of business in [REDACTED CITY], [REDACTED STATE].

5.  Defendant BETA CORP is a [REDACTED STATE] corporation with its
    principal place of business in [REDACTED CITY], [REDACTED STATE].

6.  Plaintiff is engaged in the business of manufacturing industrial
    sensors. Defendant is engaged in the business of supplying
    component parts to the aerospace industry.

                          FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS

7.  On or about [REDACTED DATE], the parties entered into a written
    Supply Agreement (the "Agreement"), under which Defendant agreed
    to manufacture and sell to Plaintiff specialised titanium alloy
    fasteners over a five-year term.

8.  The Agreement required Defendant to comply with industry
    standards AS9100D and NADCAP AC7110/12.

9.  Defendant represented in Article 4 of the Agreement that all
    fasteners would be manufactured in its [REDACTED FACILITY] plant.

10. Defendant represented in Article 7 of the Agreement that its
    quality-management system had been audited and certified as of
    [REDACTED DATE].

11. In reliance on these representations, Plaintiff placed a
    blanket purchase order on [REDACTED DATE] for $4,200,000 of
    fasteners.

12. Between [REDACTED DATE] and [REDACTED DATE], Defendant
    delivered 38 shipments of fasteners.

13. The shipments were non-conforming in two material respects:
    (a) approximately 23% of the delivered fasteners were manufactured
    at an un-audited sub-supplier facility in [REDACTED FOREIGN
    COUNTRY], not at Defendant's [REDACTED FACILITY] plant; and
    (b) the heat-treatment records supplied with the shipments were
    fabricated. The Rivera Report (Exhibit A) shows the fasteners do
    not meet the AS9100D hardness specification.

                              COUNT I — FRAUD

14. Plaintiff realleges and incorporates by reference paragraphs 1
    through 13 above as if fully set forth herein.

15. As set forth in paragraphs 9 and 10 above, Defendant made the
    following material representations of fact to Plaintiff:

    a. That the fasteners would be manufactured at its [REDACTED
       FACILITY] plant (the "Facility Representation"); and

    b. That its quality-management system had been audited and
       certified (the "Certification Representation").

16. Each of these representations was false when made.

17. Defendant knew the representations were false at the time it
    made them, or made them with reckless disregard for their
    truth.

18. Defendant made the representations with the intent that
    Plaintiff rely on them in placing the blanket purchase order
    described in paragraph 11 above.

19. Plaintiff reasonably and justifiably relied on the
    representations.

20. As a direct and proximate result of Plaintiff's reliance,
    Plaintiff has suffered damages in an amount to be proven at
    trial, but believed to exceed $4,200,000, plus interest,
    costs, and attorneys' fees.
